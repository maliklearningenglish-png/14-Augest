PAKISTAN HISTORY

THE ROAD TO INDEPENDENCE
Pakistan Movement

The Pakistan Movement was a political struggle that aimed to create a separate homeland for Muslims of the Indian subcontinent. Led by Muhammad Ali Jinnah and the All India Muslim League, it sought to protect Muslim rights and culture, eventually leading to the creation of Pakistan on 14 August 1947.

Key Events Leading to Independence

1906

Formation of the All India Muslim League

The All India Muslim League was established in Dhaka, creating a political platform for Indian Muslims to voice their concerns and protect their rights within the Indian subcontinent.

1930

Allama Iqbal's Allahabad Address

Allama Muhammad Iqbal proposed the idea of a separate Muslim state in northwestern India during his presidential address at the Allahabad session of the Muslim League.

1940

Lahore Resolution

The historic Lahore Resolution was passed at Minto Park (now Iqbal Park, Lahore), demanding independent states for Muslims in the northwestern and eastern zones of India.

1946

Direct Action Day

The Muslim League called for Direct Action Day to demand Pakistan, marking a turning point in the independence movement and demonstrating the resolve of Muslims for a separate homeland.

1947

Independence Achieved

Pakistan emerged as an independent sovereign state on 14 August 1947 after the partition of the subcontinent, fulfilling the dream of millions of Muslims.

Minar-e-Pakistan: Symbol of Freedom

Constructed in 1968 at the site of the historic Lahore Resolution, Minar-e-Pakistan stands as a national monument commemorating the struggle for Pakistan. The tower blends Mughal and modern architectural styles, symbolizing the nation's rich heritage and progressive future.

Minar-e-Pakistan in Lahore - The Tower of Pakistan

The monument stands at the exact location where the historic Lahore Resolution was passed on March 23, 1940. This resolution ultimately led to the creation of Pakistan seven years later.

Architects of Independence

Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Quaid-e-Azam

Founder of Pakistan and first Governor-General. Known as "Great Leader" for his pivotal role in the independence movement.

Allama Iqbal

Philosopher & Poet

Provided the philosophical foundation for Pakistan through his poetry and 1930 Allahabad Address.

Liaquat Ali Khan

First Prime Minister

Right-hand man of Jinnah and Pakistan's first Prime Minister who helped establish the new nation.
