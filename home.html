History of Independence

Pakistan gained independence from British rule on August 14, 1947, following a remarkable struggle led by Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah.

The journey to independence was marked by sacrifices, determination, and the unwavering spirit of millions of Muslims in the subcontinent.

Celebrations

Independence Day is celebrated with great enthusiasm across Pakistan. The day begins with flag-hoisting ceremonies and national anthem recitals.

People decorate their homes, streets, and vehicles with flags and buntings. Cultural events and fireworks light up the night sky.

National Heroes

Pakistan's independence was achieved through the sacrifices of countless heroes who dedicated their lives to the cause of a separate homeland.

From Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah to Allama Iqbal, Fatima Jinnah, and many unsung heroes, we honor their legacy and contributions.
