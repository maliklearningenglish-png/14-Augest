Our National Heores

Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah was the founder of Pakistan and leader of the All India Muslim League. Known for his vision, determination, and leadership, he united Muslims under the demand for a separate homeland. His efforts led to the creation of Pakistan on 14 August 1947, and he served as the country’s first Governor-General.

Allama Iqbal

Allama Muhammad Iqbal was a poet, philosopher, and politician who inspired the idea of a separate Muslim state. In his famous Allahabad Address of 1930, he presented the vision for an independent homeland for Muslims in northwestern India. His poetry awakened the spirit of unity, self-respect, and identity among Muslims.
